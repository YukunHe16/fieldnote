# Evidence consistency review

Use this skill to audit a document, comparison, outreach draft, or plan against its fact ledger and official requirements.

## When to use

- The user asks to review, 审校, or 核对 a draft against confirmed facts and official requirements.
- Names, dates, prompts, word limits, or citations may conflict or be unsupported.

## When not to use

- First-pass SOP or CV drafting — use statement-writing or cv-resume-writing.
- Looking up a missing official deadline or fee — use official-source-research.
- Changing saved tracker rows — use application-tracker after the user asks.

## Method

1. Check names, degree, programme, prompt, dates, requirements, and word limits.
2. Identify unsupported personal claims, contradictions, stale facts, unsupported citations, and generic text.
3. Classify findings as blocking, important, or optional, with a precise suggested fix.
4. Report what remains unverified; do not silently repair facts.

## Safety

Review is advisory: do not claim independent confirmation without sources. Do not expose sensitive source documents in summaries. Never endorse fabricated recommendation content, credentials, citations, or faculty contact.

## Evaluation prompts

- Does the review catch factual conflicts, incorrect school names, missing requirements, and unsupported claims?
- Are recommendations specific enough to act on without inventing a replacement fact?
