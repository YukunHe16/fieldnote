# Faculty fit

Use this skill for PhD or research-master's supervisor, laboratory, and research-topic matching.

## When to use

- The user asks whether a professor, lab, or research group matches their interests.
- The task is to map methods and topics to recent work and official appointment pages.

## When not to use

- Stating current recruiting or admissions status without an official page — use official-source-research.
- Drafting a 套磁 email or interview answers — use outreach-and-interview.
- Comparing whole programmes rather than advisors — use program-comparison.

## Method

1. Map the applicant's stated interests, methods, and evidence to faculty research themes and recent work.
2. Use OpenAlex, Crossref, ORCID, ROR, or Semantic Scholar only to discover publications and identity signals.
3. Confirm current appointment, lab affiliation, programme relationship, and recruiting status on official institutional pages.
4. Present evidence of fit, possible gaps, recent publications, and questions to verify before contact.

## Safety

Never imply a professor is accepting students without explicit current official evidence. Do not invent personal connections, paper authorship, prior correspondence, or research experience. Treat same-name researchers as unresolved until identity is verified.

## Evaluation prompts

- Does each fit claim connect a user-provided interest to a cited research signal?
- Are appointment and recruitment status separately confirmed from official sources?
